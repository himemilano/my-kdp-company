   - 1ヶ月目の目標数値
   - 3ヶ月目の目標数値

5.修整指示プロンプト(リテイク用)
生成した線画に問題があった時の修正指示テンプレート。よくある5つのエラーパターンに対応
⑤プロンプト本文
先ほど生成した画像に以下の修正を加えてください。該当するものだけ残して指示してください。

【A. 色が残っている場合】
The image contains colors or gray tones. Please regenerate as pure black line art on pure white background only. No shading, no gradients, no fills. Every pixel must be either pure black (#000000) for lines or pure white (#FFFFFF) for background.

【B. 線の太さが不均一】
The line weights are inconsistent. Please redraw with uniform medium-weight lines throughout. All outlines should be the same thickness, all internal detail lines should be thinner but still consistent with each other.

【C. サイズ比率が違う】
The aspect ratio is incorrect. Please regenerate with an 8.5x11 inches portrait orientation (aspect ratio 17:22). This is US Letter size for Amazon KDP paperback printing.

【D. 余白が不足】
The image extends too close to the edges. Please add at least 0.5 inch (about 12.7mm) of pure white safe zone on all four edges. Critical design elements must not be within this margin.

【E. 線が薄すぎる / 欠落している】
Some lines are broken or too faint to print clearly. Please regenerate with bold, continuous, unbroken black lines. Every line must be printable and colorable (thick enough that a child's crayon or adult's colored pencil can color within them without ambiguity).

【F. 描き込みが少ない（余白が多すぎる）】
The design has too much empty space. Please regenerate with increased detail and pattern work. Adult colorists want at least 30 minutes of engagement per page. Add more intricate background patterns, additional decorative elements, and fill large empty areas with coherent designs.

【G. 文字が混入している】
The image contains text, letters, or symbols. Please regenerate without any text, watermarks, signatures, or numbers. Pure visual imagery only.

Apply the above corrections and regenerate the image.
